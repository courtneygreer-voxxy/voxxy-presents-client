# Voxxy Presents Landing Page Audit

**Created:** February 6, 2026
**Business Focus:** Art shows, pop-up shows, and markets - needs to feel FUN and VISUAL
**Current Status:** Private Beta / Pilot Program

---

## Executive Summary

Voxxy Presents has EXCELLENT foundational copy and messaging (especially the hero text!). The site structure is solid, with clear CTAs and good navigation flow. However, it's currently lacking the visual energy needed for an art/events platform. The main opportunities are: adding real product screenshots, vibrant event imagery, and video content to bring the platform to life.

## Recommended Image Specifications

### Hero Images

- **Dimensions:** 1920x1080px (16:9)
- **Format:** WebP (with JPG fallback)
- **File size:** Under 200KB (optimized)
- **Placement:** Full-width background with dark overlay

### Product Screenshots

- **Dimensions:** 1440x900px (standard desktop view)
- **Format:** PNG (for UI clarity) or WebP
- **File size:** Under 300KB each
- **Style:** Add subtle shadow/glow, maybe slight tilt (3-5°)

### Section Background Images

- **Dimensions:** 1920x600px (wide but not too tall)
- **Format:** WebP
- **File size:** Under 150KB
- **Style:** Dark overlay (60-80% opacity) to keep text readable

### Thumbnail Images

- **Dimensions:** 400x300px
- **Format:** WebP
- **File size:** Under 50KB
- **Usage:** Feature cards, testimonial photos

### Video

- **Format:** MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080)
- **Length:** 30-90 seconds
- **File size:** Under 10MB (or use YouTube embed)

## Final Recommendations

### The Copy is GREAT - Keep It!

The hero text, problem statements, and feature descriptions are all excellent. They're specific, benefit-focused, and speak directly to pain points. DO NOT change the core messaging.

### The Design is GOOD - But Needs Energy

The structure, layout, and visual hierarchy are solid. The purple/pink gradient brand feels premium. But for a business about art shows and markets, it needs MORE:

- Color (event photos)
- Energy (videos, animations)
- Proof (product screenshots)
- Personality (custom illustrations)

### The Biggest Gap: Visual Proof

Visitors can't see the product. They're being asked to sign up for a beta without seeing the interface. Add screenshots ASAP - even if they're slightly rough or "coming soon" mockups.

### Make It Feel Like an Art Show

The business is about pop-up markets and art shows - the site should FEEL like that. Think:

- Vibrant colors
- Happy people
- Creative energy
- Community vibes
- Organized chaos becoming beautiful order
